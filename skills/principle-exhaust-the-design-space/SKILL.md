# Exhaust the Design Space

It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic.

When the user objects to a visual form, such as a table, a mark or a color, answer their question first. Then count the pages that use the old form, and show two or three cheap mock-ups on real pages before you build any of them.
