I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one. Before rewriting it, do five things:

1. Say what a top professional in this field would do here that I'm not asking for, what they would stop doing, and who else must touch the work.
2. Name the rawest material they would start from instead of what has already been digested for me. A person who can still be asked counts. Before you cut a step, name everything else that step does.
3. Name the specific methods and tools they would use.
4. Say what, other than you or me, would show the result is right. Any agent you spawn counts as you, so name a person, a source, or a run beyond that, and call agents agents when you report them.
5. Look up what you can read, then ask me in one question for the people and logistics facts the plan rests on.

Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that. Mark any example or claim you did not check against raw material as "to check".

Leave choices that are mine to make open instead of deciding them.
